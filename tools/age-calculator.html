About the Age Calculator

Age is more than just the difference of years — months and days matter for documents, exams, visas and sports categories. This calculator computes the full breakdown precisely, handling months of different lengths and leap years correctly.

Enter the date of birth and, optionally, an “as of” date (leave it blank to use today). The result shows years, months and days, the as-on date, and the total number of days lived.

Formula used

Age = full years + remaining months + remaining days, counting month lengths and leap days correctly

Results are calculated in your browser — nothing is sent to or stored on any server.

Frequently asked questions

How is age calculated exactly?

The calculator counts complete years first, then complete months, then remaining days — the standard calendar method used for official documents.

Does it handle leap years?

Yes. Birth dates like 29 February and leap-day transitions are handled correctly.

Can I calculate age on a future or past date?

Yes — set the “as of” date to any date. Leave it empty to calculate age as of today.
