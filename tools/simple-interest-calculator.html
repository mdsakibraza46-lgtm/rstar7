About the Simple Interest Calculator

Simple interest is calculated only on the original principal, unlike compound interest which also earns interest on accumulated interest. It is commonly used for short-term loans, informal lending and some fixed-income products.

Enter the principal amount, the annual interest rate and the time period in years. The calculator applies the standard simple interest formula and shows both the interest earned/charged and the total amount (principal + interest).

Formula used

SI = P × R × T / 100 where P = principal, R = annual rate (%), T = time in years | Total = P + SI

Results are calculated in your browser — nothing is sent to or stored on any server.

Frequently asked questions

How is simple interest different from compound interest?

Simple interest grows linearly — it is charged only on the principal. Compound interest grows faster because interest is also earned on previously accumulated interest.

Can I use years as a fraction?

Yes. For 18 months enter 1.5 years; for 6 months enter 0.5.

Where is simple interest used?

Short-term personal loans, vehicle title loans, some bonds and informal lending arrangements commonly use simple interest.
