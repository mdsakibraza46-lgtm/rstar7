About the Percentage Calculator

Percentages appear everywhere — bills, marks, discounts, growth reports. This calculator covers the three most common questions: “What is X% of Y?”, “X is what percent of Y?” and “What is the percentage change from X to Y?”

Pick the calculation type, enter two numbers and get the result instantly. Percentage change is shown with its sign so you can see at a glance whether it is an increase or a decrease.

Formula used

X% of Y = Y × X/100 | A is what % of B = A/B × 100 | % change = (B − A)/A × 100

Results are calculated in your browser — nothing is sent to or stored on any server.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Choose “What is B% of A”, enter the amount as A and the rate as B. Example: 18% of 2,500 = 450.

How do I find what percent X is of Y?

Choose “A is what % of B”. Example: 45 is 75% of 60.

How is percentage change calculated?

Percentage change = (new − old) / |old| × 100. Going from 80 to 100 is a +25% increase; from 80 to 60 is a −25% decrease.
