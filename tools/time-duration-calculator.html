About the Time Duration Calculator

Work shifts, study sessions, travel legs and parking durations all need the gap between two clock times. If the end time is earlier than the start time, the span is treated as crossing midnight.

Enter both times in 24-hour HH:MM format. The calculator shows hours and minutes plus the total in minutes — handy for timesheets and billing.

Formula used

Duration = end − start in minutes; if negative, 24 hours (1440 minutes) are added (overnight)

Results are calculated in your browser — nothing is sent to or stored on any server.

Frequently asked questions

How are overnight durations handled?

If the end time is earlier than the start time, the calculator adds a full day — e.g. 22:00 to 06:00 is 8 hours.

What format should I use?

24-hour HH:MM, e.g. 09:30 and 17:45. The time picker inputs enforce this automatically.

Can I get the result in total minutes?

Yes — the result includes total minutes, useful for payroll and billing calculations.
