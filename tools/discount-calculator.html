About the Discount Calculator

Discounts are usually quoted as a percentage off the original price. While simple, calculating the final price mentally — especially for odd percentages — is easy to get wrong while shopping.

This calculator multiplies the original price by the discount percentage to compute your savings, then subtracts it from the original price to show the final amount payable. Use it while shopping, billing or setting sale prices for your own products.

Formula used

Savings = price × discount% / 100 | Sale price = price − savings

Results are calculated in your browser — nothing is sent to or stored on any server.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate 30% off a price?

Multiply the price by 0.30 to get the savings, then subtract it from the original price. For ₹2,499 at 30% off, you save ₹749.70 and pay ₹1,749.30.

Can I combine two discounts?

Successive discounts multiply, they don’t add. 20% + 10% off equals 28% total off (0.8 × 0.9 = 0.72), not 30%.

Is discount calculated on MRP or selling price?

Usually on MRP (maximum retail price) or the listed price. Confirm which base the store applies the percentage to.
