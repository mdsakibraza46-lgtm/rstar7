About the Salary Calculator

Your gross salary is not what lands in your bank account. Deductions such as EPF, professional tax, insurance premiums and TDS reduce the final take-home amount.

Enter your gross monthly salary and the total of your monthly deductions. The calculator subtracts deductions to estimate your monthly take-home, and also shows the annualised gross and take-home figures for yearly planning. For a precise payroll calculation including tax slabs, consult your salary slip or payroll team.

Formula used

Take-home (monthly) = gross salary − total deductions | Annual take-home = take-home × 12

Results are calculated in your browser — nothing is sent to or stored on any server.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a deduction?

Common deductions include EPF (employee provident fund), professional tax, health insurance premiums, loan EMIs deducted at source and TDS (income tax withheld).

Is CTC the same as gross salary?

No. CTC includes employer contributions (like EPF employer share and gratuity) that never reach your hands. Gross salary is usually lower than CTC, and take-home is lower still.

Can I calculate annual figures?

Yes — the result includes annual take-home and annual gross automatically.
