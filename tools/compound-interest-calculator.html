About the Compound Interest Calculator

Compound interest means your interest also earns interest. The more frequently interest is compounded, the faster your money grows — this calculator lets you compare frequencies directly.

Enter the principal, annual rate, time in years and how many times interest compounds per year. The result shows the future value, the total interest earned and how much extra compounding frequency adds over simple annual growth.

Formula used

A = P × (1 + r/n)^(n×t) where P = principal, r = annual rate, n = compounds per year, t = years

Results are calculated in your browser — nothing is sent to or stored on any server.

Frequently asked questions

What does compounding frequency mean?

It is how many times per year interest is added to the balance. n=1 yearly, 2 half-yearly, 4 quarterly, 12 monthly, 365 daily.

Which compounding frequency gives the highest return?

For the same nominal rate, more frequent compounding yields more. Daily compounding beats monthly, which beats quarterly and yearly.

Is compound interest better than simple interest?

For savers, yes — compound interest always yields at least as much as simple interest for positive rates and periods of one year or more.
