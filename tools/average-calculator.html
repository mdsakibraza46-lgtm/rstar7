About the Average Calculator

The average (arithmetic mean) is the sum of all values divided by how many values there are. It is the most common way to summarise a set of numbers — marks, expenses, ratings, measurements.

Type or paste your numbers separated by commas (spaces also work). Invalid entries are ignored, and the calculator reports the average together with the sum and the count of valid numbers so you can double-check the input.

Formula used

Average = (sum of values) / (number of values)

Results are calculated in your browser — nothing is sent to or stored on any server.

Frequently asked questions

How do I enter the numbers?

Separate them with commas — for example: 10, 20, 30, 40. Spaces and line breaks are also accepted.

Does the order of numbers matter?

No. The average is the same regardless of order.

What is the difference between mean and median?

The mean is the total divided by count. The median is the middle value when sorted. Means are sensitive to extreme values; medians are not.
