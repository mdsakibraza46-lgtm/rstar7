About the FD Calculator

A Fixed Deposit (FD) is one of the most popular savings instruments in India — you deposit a lump sum for a fixed period and earn compound interest on it.

This calculator compounds the interest annually, the most common bank convention. Enter the deposit amount, the annual interest rate and the period in years to see the maturity value and the total interest earned. For exact quarterly-payout figures, always confirm with your bank’s FD schedule.

Formula used

Maturity = P × (1 + r)^t where P = deposit, r = annual rate, t = years (compounded yearly)

Results are calculated in your browser — nothing is sent to or stored on any server.

Frequently asked questions

How is FD interest compounded?

Most Indian banks compound FD interest quarterly or annually. This calculator uses annual compounding, the standard formula for maturity estimates.

Is FD interest taxable?

Yes. FD interest is added to your income and taxed at your income-tax slab rate. TDS may apply if interest exceeds the bank’s threshold in a financial year.

What happens if I break an FD early?

Premature withdrawal usually attracts a penalty of around 0.5–1% on the applicable rate, reducing your effective interest earnings.
