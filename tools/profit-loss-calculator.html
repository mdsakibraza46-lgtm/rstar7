About the Profit & Loss Calculator

Every seller needs a quick way to check whether a transaction is profitable. Profit is what remains when the selling price exceeds the cost price; a loss is the opposite.

This calculator shows the absolute profit or loss amount, the percentage on cost (the classic profit%/loss%) and — for profitable deals — the margin as a share of the selling price, which is how most businesses report profitability.

Formula used

Profit = SP − CP | Profit % = Profit/CP × 100 | Margin = Profit/SP × 100

Results are calculated in your browser — nothing is sent to or stored on any server.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between profit % and margin?

Profit % is calculated on the cost price (markup), while margin is calculated on the selling price. A ₹200 profit on a ₹800 cost sold at ₹1,000 is 25% markup but 20% margin.

What if cost price is zero?

Then no percentage on cost can be computed (division by zero) — the calculator shows the absolute profit only.

How do I calculate loss percentage?

Loss % = loss ÷ cost price × 100. For a cost of ₹1,000 sold at ₹750, the loss is ₹250, i.e. 25%.
