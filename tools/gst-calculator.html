About the GST Calculator

GST (Goods and Services Tax) applies at different slabs in India — commonly 5%, 12%, 18% and 28%. Sellers quote prices either exclusive of GST (base price) or inclusive of GST (final price), and converting between the two is a daily need for businesses and shoppers.

Choose “Add GST” to compute the tax on a base price, or “Remove GST” to reverse-extract the base price and tax amount from a GST-inclusive price. The result shows the GST amount and the final/base price clearly.

Formula used

Add: GST = amount × rate/100, Total = amount + GST | Remove: Base = amount / (1 + rate/100), GST = amount − Base

Results are calculated in your browser — nothing is sent to or stored on any server.

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove GST from a total price?

Choose the “Remove GST” mode. The base price equals the inclusive amount divided by (1 + rate/100). For ₹1,180 at 18% GST, the base price is ₹1,000 and GST is ₹180.

What are the current GST slabs?

The main slabs are 0%, 5%, 12%, 18% and 28%, with special rates for certain items such as gold (3%). Enter any custom rate if needed.

Is GST calculated on the base price or inclusive price?

GST always applies to the base (exclusive) price. When you only know the inclusive price, use the Remove GST mode to find the base first.
