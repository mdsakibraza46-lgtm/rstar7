About the Area Calculator

Area calculations come up in flooring, painting, gardening, land measurement and schoolwork. Each shape has its own formula, and using the wrong one is a common mistake.

Select the shape — rectangle, square, circle or triangle — and fill in the fields that appear. The calculator computes the area and displays the exact formula used, with all inputs validated so nothing silently returns a wrong number.

Formula used

Rectangle = l × w | Square = s² | Circle = π r² | Triangle = ½ × b × h

Results are calculated in your browser — nothing is sent to or stored on any server.

Frequently asked questions

Which shapes are supported?

Rectangle, square, circle and triangle — the most common needs. The relevant input fields are required per shape.

How do I calculate circle area?

Choose circle and enter the radius. Area = π × r². A circle of radius 2 has area ≈ 12.57 square units.

Are results in square metres or feet?

The result is in “square units” — whatever unit you entered. If dimensions are in metres, the area is in square metres; feet give square feet.
