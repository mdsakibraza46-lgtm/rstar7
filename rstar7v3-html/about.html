About RStar7

RStar7 is a collection of 25 free online calculators built to make everyday decisions faster and easier — from loan EMIs and SIP projections to percentages, ages and trip fuel costs.

Our mission

Most calculator websites bury you in ads, ask you to sign up, or make you guess what the numbers mean. RStar7 does the opposite: every tool is free, loads fast, works on any device and explains the formula behind the answer.

What you can calculate

Finance: EMI, loan, SIP, FD, RD, simple and compound interest, GST, discounts and salary estimates.
Math: percentages, averages, ratios, fractions and profit & loss.
Daily life: age, date differences, days between dates, time durations, BMI, fuel costs, electricity costs, tips and areas.
Converters: length units across metric and imperial systems.

Private by design

Every calculation runs entirely in your browser. The numbers you type are never sent to, or stored on, any server. There is no sign-up, no paywall and no tracking of what you calculate.

Free to use, always

RStar7 is supported by unobtrusive advertising. All tools remain completely free for everyone, on every device, with no account required.
