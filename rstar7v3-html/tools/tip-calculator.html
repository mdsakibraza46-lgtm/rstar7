About the Tip Calculator

Tipping conventions vary — 5% for modest service, 10% for good service and upwards for excellent service, depending on where you are and whether service charge is already on the bill.

Enter the bill amount, tip percentage and the number of people sharing. The calculator shows the tip amount, the total with tip and what each person should pay.

Formula used

Tip = bill × tip% / 100 | Per person = (bill + tip) ÷ people

Results are calculated in your browser — nothing is sent to or stored on any server.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I tip?

Common practice is 5–10% in India where service charges may already apply, and 15–20% in the US. Check your bill for a “service charge” line before adding a tip.

How is the bill split per person?

The bill plus tip is divided equally by the number of people. For a ₹2,000 bill with 10% tip and 4 people: ₹2,200 ÷ 4 = ₹550 each.

What if I don’t want to tip?

Enter 0 as the tip percentage — the calculator then just splits the bill.
