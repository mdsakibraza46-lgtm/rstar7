About the SIP Calculator

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) lets you invest a fixed amount in a mutual fund every month. Because each instalment compounds over time, even small monthly amounts can grow into a large corpus over long periods.

This calculator projects the maturity value of your SIP using the standard future-value-of-annuity-due formula. It also shows the total amount you invested and the estimated gain, so you can see the power of compounding at a glance. Remember that mutual fund returns are not guaranteed — the result is a projection based on the return rate you enter.

Formula used

Maturity = P × ((1+i)^n − 1) / i × (1+i) where P = monthly investment, i = monthly return (annual ÷ 12), n = total months

Results are calculated in your browser — nothing is sent to or stored on any server.

Frequently asked questions

What return rate should I assume?

Historically, diversified equity mutual funds in India have delivered around 10–12% annualised over long periods, while debt funds deliver less. Choose a conservative rate for planning.

Is the SIP amount invested at the start of each month?

Yes — this calculator assumes each instalment is invested at the beginning of the month (annuity-due), which is the common convention for SIP projections.

Are SIP returns guaranteed?

No. SIPs invest in market-linked mutual funds, so actual returns vary. Use this calculator as an estimate, not a guarantee.
