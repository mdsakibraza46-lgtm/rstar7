About the Electricity Cost Calculator

Electricity is billed in kilowatt-hours (kWh), commonly called “units”. Your bill is essentially units consumed multiplied by the rate per unit, plus fixed charges and duties that vary by provider.

Enter the units consumed (from your meter or an appliance estimate) and your per-unit rate to get the energy cost. Use it to check appliance running costs too: a 1.5 kW AC run for 8 hours uses roughly 12 units.

Formula used

Cost = units (kWh) × rate per unit

Results are calculated in your browser — nothing is sent to or stored on any server.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find units consumed?

Read your electricity meter (difference from last reading), or estimate: appliance wattage × hours used ÷ 1000 = units. A 100W bulb for 10 hours uses 1 unit.

Does this include fixed charges?

No — this computes the energy cost only (units × rate). Add your provider’s fixed charges, wheeling charges and duties separately if needed.

What is a typical per-unit rate in India?

Domestic tariffs commonly range from about ₹4 to ₹10 per unit depending on the state and consumption slab. Use the rate printed on your latest bill.
