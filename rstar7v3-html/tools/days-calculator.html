About the Days Calculator

Adding or subtracting days is a common task: invoice due dates, return windows, delivery estimates, exam countdowns. Doing it across month boundaries is error-prone by hand.

Choose a date, pick “Add” or “Subtract”, enter the number of days and calculate. You get the resulting date plus the day of the week, so you immediately know if it lands on a weekend.

Formula used

Result = date ± N days, adjusted across months and years automatically

Results are calculated in your browser — nothing is sent to or stored on any server.

Frequently asked questions

Can I subtract days from a date?

Yes — choose “Subtract” mode. For example, 20 days before 10 January 2024 is 21 December 2023.

Does it cross month and year boundaries?

Yes — the calculation uses the calendar, so adding 20 days to 10 Jan 2024 correctly lands on 30 Jan 2024, across any month or year boundary.

Will it tell me the weekday?

Yes — the result includes the day of the week for the resulting date.
