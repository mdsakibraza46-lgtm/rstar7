About the Loan Calculator

Before borrowing, it pays to know the full cost of a loan — not just the monthly payment. Two loans with the same EMI can have very different total interest depending on the rate and tenure.

Enter the loan amount, annual interest rate and tenure in months. The calculator shows the estimated monthly instalment using the standard reducing-balance method, the total repayment over the entire period and the total interest you will pay.

Formula used

Monthly payment = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1) | Total repayment = payment × n | Total interest = total repayment − P

Results are calculated in your browser — nothing is sent to or stored on any server.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between loan amount and total repayment?

The loan amount (principal) is what you borrow. The total repayment is the principal plus all interest charged over the tenure — the actual money that leaves your pocket.

Does a longer tenure mean a cheaper loan?

A longer tenure lowers your monthly payment but increases the total interest paid over the life of the loan. A shorter tenure costs more per month but less overall.

Is this the same formula banks use?

Yes — the calculator uses the standard reducing-balance EMI formula used by banks and NBFCs for retail loans.
