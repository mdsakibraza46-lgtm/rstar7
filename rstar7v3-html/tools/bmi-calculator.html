About the BMI Calculator

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a quick screening measure that relates weight to height. The WHO classifies BMI below 18.5 as underweight, 18.5–24.9 as normal weight, 25–29.9 as overweight and 30 or above as obese.

Enter your weight in kg and height in cm. The calculator shows your BMI value, the category it falls into and the healthy BMI range. Note that BMI does not distinguish muscle from fat, so athletes may read high while being healthy — use it as a starting point, not a diagnosis.

Formula used

BMI = weight (kg) / height² (m²)

Results are calculated in your browser — nothing is sent to or stored on any server.

Frequently asked questions

How is BMI calculated?

BMI = weight in kilograms divided by the square of height in metres. For 70 kg at 175 cm: 70 ÷ (1.75 × 1.75) ≈ 22.9.

What is a healthy BMI?

The WHO healthy range is 18.5 to 24.9. Below 18.5 is underweight, 25–29.9 is overweight, and 30+ is classified as obese.

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

BMI is a useful screening tool but has limits — it does not account for muscle mass, bone density, age or body composition. Consult a professional for a full assessment.
