About the Fuel Cost Calculator

Trip fuel cost depends on three things: distance, fuel efficiency (km per litre) and the price of petrol or diesel. This calculator multiplies them out and also shows the fuel needed and the effective cost per kilometre.

Use it to split travel costs among friends, compare two-wheelers and cars, or budget road trips. For accuracy, use your vehicle’s real-world mileage rather than the brochure figure.

Formula used

Litres needed = distance ÷ mileage | Fuel cost = litres × price per litre

Results are calculated in your browser — nothing is sent to or stored on any server.

Frequently asked questions

How is fuel cost calculated?

Litres needed = distance ÷ mileage (km per litre). Cost = litres × price per litre. A 300 km trip at 15 km/l and ₹105/l needs 20 litres, costing ₹2,100.

Can I use this for diesel and CNG?

Yes — enter the price per litre/kg of whatever fuel your vehicle uses, and its efficiency in km per litre (or kg).

Why do my real costs differ slightly?

Traffic, AC usage, terrain and driving style change real-world mileage. Use your own measured average for the best estimate.
