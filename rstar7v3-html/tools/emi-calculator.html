About the EMI Calculator

An EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment) is the fixed amount you pay every month to repay a loan. Each payment covers both the principal and the interest charged by the lender. Knowing your EMI before taking a loan helps you choose a tenure and loan amount that comfortably fits your monthly budget.

This calculator uses the standard reducing-balance formula used by banks and NBFCs in India. Enter the loan amount, the annual interest rate offered to you and the tenure in months — the calculator instantly shows your monthly EMI, the total amount payable over the full tenure and the total interest component.

Formula used

EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1) where P = loan amount, r = monthly interest rate (annual ÷ 12), n = tenure in months

Results are calculated in your browser — nothing is sent to or stored on any server.

Frequently asked questions

What is an EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment — the fixed payment you make each month towards repaying a loan. It includes both a portion of the principal amount and the interest.

How is EMI calculated?

EMI is calculated using the formula P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1), where P is the loan principal, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of monthly instalments.

How can I reduce my EMI?

You can reduce your EMI by choosing a longer tenure, negotiating a lower interest rate, making a larger down payment, or making part-prepayments during the loan period.
