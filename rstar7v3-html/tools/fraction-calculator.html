About the Fraction Calculator

Working with fractions by hand means finding common denominators and simplifying — easy to slip up. This calculator performs all four operations on two fractions and reduces the result to lowest terms automatically.

Enter the numerators and denominators of both fractions, choose the operation and calculate. Results are shown in simplest form; improper results are additionally displayed as mixed numbers (e.g. 3 3/4).

Formula used

a/b + c/d = (ad + cb)/bd | a/b − c/d = (ad − cb)/bd | a/b × c/d = ac/bd | a/b ÷ c/d = ad/bc

Results are calculated in your browser — nothing is sent to or stored on any server.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add two fractions?

Enter both fractions and choose “+”. The calculator cross-multiplies over the common denominator and simplifies. Example: 1/2 + 1/3 = 5/6.

What is a mixed number?

A mixed number shows an improper fraction as a whole plus a proper fraction, e.g. 15/4 = 3 3/4. Both forms are displayed when applicable.

Can denominators be negative or zero?

Denominators must be whole numbers of at least 1. A zero denominator is mathematically undefined and is rejected with a clear message.
