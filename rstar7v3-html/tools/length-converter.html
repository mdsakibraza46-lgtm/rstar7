About the Length Converter

Length conversion is needed in construction, tailoring, travel, sports and online shopping. Metric and imperial units don’t convert by round numbers, so mental math is unreliable.

Enter a value, choose the unit to convert from and the unit to convert to. The converter uses exact standard factors (1 inch = 0.0254 m, 1 mile = 1609.344 m, etc.) and also shows the intermediate value in metres.

Formula used

Value in target unit = value × (factor of source ÷ factor of target), factors relative to 1 metre

Results are calculated in your browser — nothing is sent to or stored on any server.

Frequently asked questions

Which units are supported?

Millimetre (mm), centimetre (cm), metre (m), kilometre (km), inch (in), foot (ft), yard (yd) and mile (mi).

How many feet are in a metre?

One metre is 1 ÷ 0.3048 ≈ 3.28 feet. The converter handles this and every other pair automatically.

Are the conversion factors exact?

Yes — internationally agreed exact factors are used, e.g. 1 inch = 25.4 mm exactly and 1 mile = 1609.344 m exactly.
