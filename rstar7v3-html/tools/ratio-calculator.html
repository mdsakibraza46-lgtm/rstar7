About the Ratio Calculator

A ratio expresses how two quantities compare. Simplifying a ratio means dividing both sides by their greatest common divisor so the relationship stays the same but the numbers are as small as possible — 16:10 becomes 8:5.

Enter any two positive numbers, including decimals such as 0.5 : 1.5. The calculator scales decimal values to whole numbers, divides by the GCD and shows the simplest equivalent ratio.

Formula used

Simplified ratio = (a ÷ gcd(a,b)) : (b ÷ gcd(a,b))

Results are calculated in your browser — nothing is sent to or stored on any server.

Frequently asked questions

How is a ratio simplified?

Both numbers are divided by their greatest common divisor (GCD). 16 and 10 share GCD 2, so 16:10 simplifies to 8:5.

Can I simplify decimal ratios?

Yes — values like 0.5 : 1.5 are scaled to whole numbers first (5 : 15) and then simplified to 1 : 3.

What is the difference between a ratio and a fraction?

A ratio compares two quantities (a:b); a fraction represents a part of a whole (a/b). Simplification uses the same GCD idea for both.
