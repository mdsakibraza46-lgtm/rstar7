About the RD Calculator

A Recurring Deposit (RD) lets you deposit a fixed sum every month and earn interest on it, making it ideal for salaried savers building a corpus gradually.

This calculator uses the widely used RD approximation formula that values each monthly instalment for the number of months it stays invested. It shows the total deposited, the estimated interest and the approximate maturity value. Banks that compound quarterly may credit slightly different interest — use the result as a close estimate.

Formula used

Maturity ≈ R × n + R × n(n+1)/2 × i where R = monthly deposit, n = months, i = monthly rate (annual ÷ 12)

Results are calculated in your browser — nothing is sent to or stored on any server.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the result called approximate?

The formula is a standard approximation that assumes simple monthly interest accumulation. Banks compounding quarterly credit interest slightly differently, so the exact maturity can differ marginally.

What is the minimum RD tenure?

Most banks offer RDs from 6 months to 10 years. Enter any tenure in months to estimate its maturity.

Is RD interest taxable?

Yes — RD interest is taxable as per your income-tax slab, and TDS applies above the bank’s specified interest threshold.
